Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Don't Trust Anyone (dta.inkfinitystudio.store), published by Inkfinity Studio, handles data, cookies, and advertising.

← Back to Game

1. Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads and keep the game free to play. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google Partner Sites Privacy.
  • For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, consent preferences are managed via a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) when personalized or non-personalized ads are served.

2. Game Progress, Accounts & Multiplayer

  • Local Storage: Level unlocks, death counts, audio settings, and selected outfits are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.
  • Optional Sign-In: If you choose to sign in, authentication is handled via Firebase Authentication to sync your profile, coins, and unlocked outfits.
  • 2-Player Online Co-op: Room codes, player display names, and in-game chat messages are relayed in real time over WebSockets to run your co-op session and are not sold to third parties.

3. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of account data, contact Inkfinity Studio at support@inkfinitystudio.store.
