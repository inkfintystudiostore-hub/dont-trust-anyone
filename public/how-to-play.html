Play With Friends Online (No Download)

  1. Open dta.inkfinitystudio.store on desktop or mobile.
  2. Select 2-Player Co-op from the main menu.
  3. Create a room and copy your 9-digit room code or invite link.
  4. Send it to your friend—once they join, press Start to begin the 15 Couple Co-op levels together.

Core Rules & Troll Mechanics

  • Don't trust anything: Floors vanish, spikes hide behind checkpoints, and fake "LEVEL COMPLETE!" banners try to bait you.
  • Shared-fate Co-op: In 2-player mode, both players must reach the exit door together. If either player dies, both restart the level.
  • Keys & Bombs: Some co-op stages require collecting a Golden Key or defusing a bomb before the exit gate opens.

Controls

  • Move: ← → or A D
  • Jump: Space or W / ↑
  • Fast-Fall: ↓ or S
  • Restart / Map / Hint: R restart · M level map · H hint
  • Mobile: Built-in on-screen touch joystick and jump button.
