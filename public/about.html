About Don't Trust Anyone

Don't Trust Anyone is an original 3D browser troll platformer created by Inkfinity Studio. Built with Three.js and Cloudflare Workers, it runs directly in desktop and mobile web browsers with zero downloads.

Play Free Now →

What Makes It Different

  • 62 Handcrafted Levels: Explore 47 Solo stages across Candy Meadow, Toy Factory, Haunted Bakery, and Mind Games, plus 15 Couple Co-op stages.
  • Real-Time 2-Player Online Co-op: Create a private room with a 9-digit code or invite link. Both players share fate—if one falls into a trap, both restart together.
  • Deceptive Trap Design: Expect disappearing floors, fake win banners, hidden spikes, gravity flips, and playful misdirection.
  • Cross-Device Controls: Play with keyboard shortcuts on desktop or the built-in touch joystick and action buttons on mobile.
