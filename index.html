Don't Trust Anyone — Free 3D Troll Platformer

Play Don't Trust Anyone free online in your browser with no download required. Navigate 62 deceptive 3D levels solo or in 2-player online co-op.

Key Features

  • 100% free to play in desktop and mobile browsers — zero download or install.
  • 62 handcrafted 3D troll platformer levels across 5 worlds: Candy Meadow, Toy Factory, Haunted Bakery, Mind Games, and Couple Co-op.
  • 47 Solo levels and 15 2-Player Online Co-op levels with room codes, invite links, and in-game chat.
  • Deceptive traps: fake win screens, fake checkpoints, disappearing floors, and hidden spikes.
  • 8 unlockable 3D character outfits: Momo, Naruto, Gojo, Goku, Doraemon, Princess, Dumpling, and Fairy.

Rules & Controls

  • Solo Mode: Reach the exit door while surviving fake signs, moving spikes, and gravity flips.
  • 2-Player Co-op Mode: Both players must reach the exit door together. If one player dies, both restart the level.
  • Desktop Controls: Arrow keys or A/D to move, Down arrow for fast-fall, Space to jump, R to restart, M for level map, H for hint.
  • Mobile Controls: On-screen touch joystick and jump/restart buttons.
