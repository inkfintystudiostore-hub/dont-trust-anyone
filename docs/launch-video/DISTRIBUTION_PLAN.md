# 10,000 Players: launch distribution plan

> Seedhi baat: koi bhi ad akele 10,000 players ki guarantee nahi deta. Players tab aate hain jab sahi video sahi logon tak baar baar pahunche, aur game ka link ek tap mein khule. Ye plan usi ke liye hai.

## Funnel ka hisaab (andaza, guarantee nahi)

| Step | Typical rate (short-form, free browser game) | 10k players ke liye chahiye |
|---|---|---|
| Video views → profile/link tap | 1–3% | ~5–10 lakh views |
| Link tap → game khula aur khela | 40–60% (browser, no install) | ~17–25k taps |

Matlab target ~**5–10 lakh views** hai, 30–45 din mein. Ye ek video se nahi, **15–25 posts** ki series se aata hai.

## 1. Pehle hafte se pehle (setup)

- **Ek short link:** `inkfinitystudio.store/game-room` theek hai. Har platform ke liye `?src=ig`, `?src=yt`, `?src=wa` jaisa tag lagao, taaki pata chale players kahan se aaye.
- **Pinned comment / bio link** har platform pe.
- **Room link share** hi growth engine hai: har 2-player game ek naye player ko laata hai. Abhi link sirf Room screen pe milta hai. Solo end screen pe bhi "Dost ko challenge karo" wala share button lagaya jaaye to har player naya player la sakta hai. (Ye game mein abhi nahi hai, alag kaam hai.)

## 2. Content series (isi footage se, alag-alag hooks)

| # | Video | Hook | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Master ad (ye wala)** | "Ye game dost ke saath mat khelna" | 30s |
| 2 | Solo rage | "Duniya ka sabse aasaan game" → Level 1 pe maut | 15s |
| 3 | "Is sign pe bharosa karoge?" | Freeze frame, comment mein jawab | 10s |
| 4–10 | **Ek level = ek video** | Har trap ka reveal: kela, murgi, coin, bhaagta darwaza… | 8–12s |
| 11 | Couples | "Girlfriend ke saath khela… breakup ho gaya" | 15s |
| 12 | Challenge | "Level 1 bina mare paar karke dikhao" (duet/stitch ke liye) | 10s |
| 13+ | Players ke clips | Logon ke rage reactions repost (permission ke saath) | — |

Rule: **har din 1 post**, pehle 2 hafte. Jo video chal jaaye, usi format ke 3 aur variants banao.

## 3. Platforms (priority order)

1. **Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts:** same 9:16 file. Hindi/Hinglish captions, trending audio ki jagah apna SFX + music.
2. **Moj / Josh / ShareChat:** Hindi-first audience, rage-bait humour wahan bahut chalta hai.
3. **WhatsApp status + groups:** 15s cutdown + room link. 2-player game ke liye ye sabse sasta channel hai.
4. **Reddit:** r/IndianGaming, r/WebGames, r/IndieGaming. Ek GIF + "I made a troll platformer you can play in the browser", aur link comment mein.
5. **itch.io + Newgrounds + CrazyGames/Poki submission:** browser games ke portals, jinpe roz ka free traffic aata hai.

## 4. Creators (sabse tez raasta)

- 20–30 **chhote Hindi gaming creators** (10k–100k followers) ko DM karo: "Free browser game hai, dost ke saath khel ke reaction banao." Rage games reaction content ke liye perfect hain.
- 2 creators bhi agar couple ke saath khel ke video daalein, to akele wahi 10k players la sakte hain.

## 5. Paid boost (optional, chhota budget)

- Jo organic video sabse achha chale (3s hold rate aur shares sabse zyada), sirf usi ko Instagram pe boost karo. 18–30 age, India, interest "mobile games / gaming".
- Pehle ₹500/din × 5 din ka test, phir cost per link tap dekh ke badhao.

## 6. Har hafte kya naapna hai

- Views, 3-second hold %, shares, link taps (har `?src=` tag ke hisaab se).
- Game mein: kitne naye players aaye, kitne 2-player rooms bane, Level 1 pe kitne ruk gaye.
- Jo hook jeete, wahi agle hafte doubled.
