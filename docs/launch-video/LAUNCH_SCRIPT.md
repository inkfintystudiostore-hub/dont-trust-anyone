## 1. Research: ye game kyon bikega

| Asli cheez game mein | Video mein kaise use karein |
|---|---|
| Har trap chhupa hai, koi warning nahi | Viewer ko bhi dhoka do. Pehle 2 sec mein "aasaan" dikhao, phir maaro. |
| Game khud taunt karta hai ("Level 1 hai bhai. LEVEL 1.") | Ye lines seedhe on-screen text ban jaayengi. Likhne ki zaroorat hi nahi. |
| Skull death counter | Corner mein chalta raho: 1 → 47 → 99+. Ye "main kitni baar marunga?" wali curiosity jagata hai. |
| Momo + Mimi, 2-player room, ek mara to **dono** restart | Sabse shareable angle: "dost ko bhejo, dosti khatam." |
| 42 levels, 5 worlds, Hinglish level names | Montage mein level names flash: "Kele Ka Mausam", "Murgi Mat Dabao", "Dimaag Ki Dahi". |
| Browser mein chalta hai, link se room join | CTA simple: "Link kholo, khelo." Koi download nahi. |

**Audience feel target:** 70% "ARRGH!" + 30% "hahaha", jo GAME_PLAN ka feel target bhi hai. Viewer ko lage *"main isse better kar lunga"*. Isi ego se click aata hai.

**Silent-first rule:** zyada log Reels/Shorts bina awaaz dekhte hain. Isliye har beat ka text screen pe hoga, aur awaaz bonus rahegi.

## 2. Hook options (pehle 2 second)

### Hook A: "Sabse aasaan game" ⭐ recommended
- **Frame 1:** Momo seedhe floor pe chal raha hai, upar text: **"Duniya ka sabse AASAAN game 😇"**
- **1.2 sec:** floor gayab, Momo gira. Hard cut, record-scratch SFX, music band.
- **Text:** "Level 1 hai bhai. **LEVEL 1.**" (ye game ki apni line hai)
- **Kyon kaam karta hai:** jo vaada kiya wahi 1 sec mein toota. Isse pattern interrupt banta hai aur viewer hasta hai. Retention ke liye best.

### Hook B: "Tum hote to jaate?"
- Freeze frame pe "Safe ➜" wala sign, text: **"Is sign pe bharosa karoge? 🤔"**
- 1.5 sec ruko, phir pul toot jaata hai.
- **Kyon:** viewer ko faisla lene pe majboor karta hai, aur comments mein "main nahi jaata" jaisa engagement aata hai.

### Hook C: "Dost ko bhejo" (2-player cutdown ke liye)
- Momo aur Mimi saath. Ek trap chhuta, dono mare. Text: **"Ye game dost ke saath mat khelna."**
- Neeche chhota text: "…ek mara to dono marte hain 💀"
- **Kyon:** share karne ka reason khud video de raha hai ("isko tag karo").

**✅ Chuna gaya: Hook C** (16 Nov). Master video ab dost/2-player angle se shuru hota hai. Hook A solo cutdown mein jaayega.
